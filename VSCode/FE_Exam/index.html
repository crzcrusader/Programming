FE Exam Essentials

  1. Search Terms Matter: Don't scroll through the PDF. Practice searching keywords like "Euler," "Bending," "Bernoulli," or "Present Worth."
  2. Units Check: Double-check unit conversions (e.g., kips to lbs, inches to feet, kW to W). Over 30% of wrong answers are unit-mismatch traps.
  3. Pacing Strategy: You have roughly 3 minutes per question (110 questions in 5 hours and 20 minutes). Flag time-consuming questions and move on.
  4. Guessing Penalty: There is no penalty for incorrect answers. Never leave a question blank on test day.
